The Studio — Oslo & London

Erik &
Marte

Bjorn & Strand was founded in 2008 by Erik Bjorn and Marte Strand, two architects who met at the Oslo School of Architecture and shared a single conviction — that the best buildings are the quietest ones.

Erik Bjorn & Marte Strand
Oslo Studio, 2024
Erik
Bjorn
Founding Partner — Architecture

Erik leads the studio's architectural projects, with a particular focus on residential work and the relationship between building and landscape. His approach is patient and material-led — he believes a building should be understood through touch as much as sight.

Before founding Bjorn & Strand, he worked with Snøhetta in Oslo and David Chipperfield Architects in London.

Education
Oslo School of Architecture — Master of Architecture, 2003
ETH Zürich — Exchange, 2002
Marte
Strand
Founding Partner — Interiors

Marte leads the studio's interior and cultural projects. Where Erik thinks about structure and permanence, Marte thinks about inhabitation — how a space changes with the hour of day, the season, the age of the people living in it.

She studied architecture in Oslo before completing a second degree in furniture design in Copenhagen, where she developed her understanding of the interior as an extension of architecture.

Education
Oslo School of Architecture — Master of Architecture, 2004
Royal Danish Academy — Furniture Design, 2006
What We Believe

How we
think

Every decision we make — from the orientation of a building on its site to the radius of a door handle — comes from the same set of convictions we've held since we started.

  • 01
    Restraint is a skill

    The hardest thing in architecture is knowing what to leave out. We take on fewer projects so we can give each one more thought — and more time to find what isn't needed.

  • 02
    Materials age

    We choose materials for how they behave over decades, not how they photograph on day one. Timber that silvers. Stone that wears. Brass that patinas. Buildings that earn their beauty.

  • 03
    Light is the material

    Every project begins with a study of light — how it enters, how it moves, what it reveals at different times of day. Architecture that doesn't consider light isn't finished.

  • 04
    The client lives there

    We are not making monuments. We are making places for people to live and work in. Their needs, habits, and preferences matter more than our preferences as architects.

The Studio in Numbers

Proud of what
we've built

2008 Year Founded

Established in Oslo by Erik Bjorn and Marte Strand after returning from practices in London and Zürich. We have been independent ever since.

80+ Projects Completed

Across residential, commercial, cultural and interior typologies in six countries — from private lakeside homes to public cultural institutions.

6 Awards Won

Including the Norwegian Design Award, Wallpaper* Design Award, and a Dezeen Award for Interior of the Year — recognition we are genuinely proud of.

12 People in the Studio

A deliberately small team of architects, interior designers, and one very patient model-maker. Small enough to care about every detail.

3 Studio Locations

Oslo, London, and Copenhagen — each chosen for the clients, landscapes, and creative communities they give us access to.

15+ Years in Practice

Over fifteen years of building slowly, carefully, and only when we believe in the project. Longevity in architecture comes from saying no as often as yes.
